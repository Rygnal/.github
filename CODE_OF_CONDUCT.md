# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Rygnal is committed to a respectful, focused, and professional open-source environment.

We want contributors, maintainers, and users to be able to participate in good faith,
get technical work done, and be treated with basic respect — regardless of background,
experience level, or opinion.

---

## Expected behavior

Participants in Rygnal spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, and any
other project venue — are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive in all interactions
- Give clear, specific, and actionable technical feedback
- Assume good intent and ask for clarification before assuming bad faith
- Stay focused on the project, its goals, and the problem at hand
- Respect maintainers' time, decisions, and bandwidth
- Acknowledge and address valid feedback, even when it disagrees with your approach
- Be patient with contributors at different experience levels

---

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable in Rygnal spaces:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks of any kind
- Discriminatory language or conduct based on race, gender, sexual orientation,
  disability, nationality, religion, or any other personal characteristic
- Trolling, deliberate provocation, or repeated off-topic disruption
- Publishing private or personal information about others without explicit permission
- Abusive, demeaning, or dismissive comments in issues, pull requests, or discussions
- Sustained unwelcome contact after someone has asked you to stop
- Conduct that a reasonable person would find hostile or professionally inappropriate

---

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

They may take any action they consider appropriate in response to behavior that violates
these standards, including:

- Editing or removing comments, commits, issues, or other contributions
- Closing issues or pull requests
- Temporarily or permanently restricting participation
- Blocking users from the organization

Enforcement actions will be proportionate to the severity and pattern of the behavior.

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness conduct that violates this Code of Conduct, report it
privately to the project maintainers.

When reporting, include:
- A description of what happened
- Relevant links, screenshots, or context
- Whether you prefer to remain anonymous

Reports will be handled confidentially. Maintainers who are the subject of a report
will not participate in reviewing it.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all Rygnal project spaces, including GitHub repositories,
issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other venues where Rygnal project work occurs.
